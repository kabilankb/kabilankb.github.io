Simulate & teleoperate

Digital twins and teleop rigs that turn real arms into training data.

P01Simulation + teleoperation

YAMLab on Isaac Sim 6 with SO-101 leader-arm teleoperation

Ported a bimanual YAM robot framework to Isaac Lab 3.0 and drove both simulated arms with real SO-101 leader arms.

  • Two leaders drive both arms, or one leader switches between them
  • Teleop sessions record to HDF5 and replay into LeRobot datasets with 3 camera views
  • PutPotOnCooktop pick-and-place replays successfully at ~40 env-steps/s on 4 RGB envs
  • Isaac Sim 6.0.1
  • Isaac Lab 3.0
  • PyTorch 2.10
  • LeRobot
github.com/kabilankb/Isaac-Lab-3-and-add-SO-101-leader-teleoperation

P03Real-to-sim bridge

rebot-isaacsim-bridge

A real-time bridge that mirrors a real arm's LeRobot teleop session into Isaac Sim, without touching real-arm control.

  • Streams follower joint state every loop iteration over a simple UDP JSON protocol
  • Per-joint sign flips and gripper scaling match any simulated asset's conventions
  • Supports Seeed reBotArm B601 follower and 102 leader; pip-installable sender
  • LeRobot
  • Isaac Sim
  • UDP / JSON
  • Seeed reBotArm B601
github.com/kabilankb/rebot-isaacsim-bridge

P09Mobile manipulation + teleoperation

XLeRobot IsaacLab

A standalone Isaac Lab extension for the XLeRobot dual-arm mobile manipulator, with keyboard and gamepad teleoperation.

  • Three environments: ground plane, cube lifting and a loft scene
  • Drive the base at three speeds and control both arms and grippers from keyboard or gamepad
  • Runs up to 4 parallel environments; URDF and USD assets ship with the repo
  • Isaac Sim 4.5+
  • Isaac Lab
  • URDF / USD
  • Keyboard + Xbox gamepad
github.com/kabilankb/xlerobot_isaaclab-

P10VR teleoperation

SO-101 VR Control

Open-source, browser-based VR teleoperation for the SO-101 arm with a Meta Quest: one command, no app install.

  • Hold grip and the end effector follows your hand; trigger closes the gripper
  • PyBullet inverse kinematics drives six STS3215 servos through LeRobot
  • Safety: 200°/s velocity limit, 10° per-command cap, 5° joint margins
  • Meta Quest
  • WebXR + A-Frame
  • WebSocket (WSS)
  • PyBullet IK
  • LeRobot
github.com/kabilankb/SO-101-VR-Control

P04Reinforcement learning

isaaclab_microduck

Isaac Lab 3.0 RL environments for the Microduck bipedal robot, built on Newton's MuJoCo-Warp solver.

  • BallKick and BallRally trained and measured; 149 CPU-only config tests
  • Isaac Lab 3.0
  • Newton (MuJoCo-Warp)
  • PyTorch
  • ONNX

newton-claw-thor

P11Agentic code-as-policiesNew · Oct 2026

ASPIRE × YAM gear assembly in Isaac Sim

Runs NVIDIA ASPIRE on bimanual YAM arms in Isaac Lab. An AI agent writes the robot's control program in Python, runs it, reads the failures and fixes the code, then turns lessons that hold up into verified, reusable skills. The task: pick up a factory gear and seat it on the middle shaft.

  • A bridge serves the Isaac Lab task to ASPIRE: arm commands, RGB-D cameras, seeded resets and task success
  • Round 1 seats the gear on 6 of 10 clean held-out seeds; a separate verifier agent confirmed all 5 new skills
  • Round 2's tilted reach solves 3 of 9 far-shaft seeds, where round 1 solved none
  • NVIDIA ASPIRE
  • Isaac Sim 6
  • Isaac Lab 3.0
  • YAM bimanual
  • SAM3
  • mink IK
github.com/kabilankb/aspire-yam-gear-assembly

P06VLA fine-tuning + benchmarking

SO-101 Bench × GR00T-N1.7

Fine-tune and evaluate GR00T-N1.7 on SO-101 Bench in an Isaac Lab digital twin, with no physical robot required.

  • Language-conditioned tabletop suite over 56 household objects
  • Loss 1.114 → 0.065 over 20k steps in ~90 min on a single RTX PRO 6000
  • Automatic episode scoring with failure tags such as failed_grasp and wrong_object
  • Isaac Sim / Isaac Lab 5.1
  • GR00T-N1.7-3B
  • LeRobot
  • ZMQ
github.com/kabilankb/so101-bench-groot-n1.7

P05Policy serving

gb10_cosmos_policy_server

A Docker setup that serves the Cosmos3-Nano action policy against the RoboLab benchmark on NVIDIA GB10 hardware.

  • Containerized policy server; the RoboLab client can run any of 120 registered tasks
  • Patched for GB10: PyTorch attention fallback and a PyAV video decoder
  • Fine-tuning pipeline: DCP conversion, dry-run-validated SFT configs, export for serving
  • NVIDIA GB10 (Grace Blackwell)
  • CUDA 13.0
  • Docker
  • Cosmos3-Nano
  • RoboLab
github.com/kabilankb/gb10_cosmos_policy_server

P02Edge deployment · hardware-in-the-loopFeatured on Jetson AI Lab

Cosmos3-Edge SO-101 on Jetson Thor

Runs a ~2B Cosmos3-Edge action policy on Jetson Thor and closes the control loop with an Isaac Lab digital twin on a laptop.

  • Policy returns 32-step × 6-joint action chunks, streamed to a live web dashboard
  • torch.compile + cuDNN attention cut chunk time from 4.3 s to ~1.5–2.1 s
  • Split architecture: sim and UI on a laptop, inference on Thor over SSH + msgpack
  • Jetson AGX Thor
  • JetPack 7
  • Cosmos3-Edge
  • Isaac Lab 3.0
  • WebSocket
github.com/kabilankb/so101-cosmos-nano-policy/tree/cosmos-edge/edge/thor-hil

P08World foundation model · GB10 + Jetson Thor

Cosmos3-Nano on NVIDIA GB10

Runs NVIDIA's 16B-parameter Cosmos3-Nano world model on Blackwell aarch64 devices, behind a Gradio generator UI.

  • Text-to-video and image-to-video: 57 frames at 480p in ~3 min on GB10
  • Text-to-image in ~30 s, with output up to 1280×720
  • ~33 GB model fits in GB10's 128 GB unified memory and loads in ~5 s
  • GB10
  • Jetson Thor
  • CUDA 13.0
  • PyTorch 2.12
  • Diffusers
  • Gradio
