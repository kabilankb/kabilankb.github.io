DreamZero-DROID on one Jetson AGX Orin

Hardware in the loop · World-action model on the edge

NVIDIA's DreamZero-DROID, a 14B world-action model meant for 2× H100 or a GB200, running as a policy server on a single Jetson AGX Orin 64GB. A laptop simulates the DROID Franka in Isaac Sim 6, streams its three cameras to the Orin over a websocket and executes the 24-step action chunks that come back.

Getting it to fit took a streaming bf16 loader, freeing the 11.4 GB text encoder after the prompt is cached, a gloo process group instead of NCCL, and a port of the DROID sim from Isaac Sim 5 to Isaac Sim 6 / Isaac Lab 3. Every change is an opt-in environment variable on a pinned DreamZero checkout.

47 GBafter load, bf16, down from a ~140 GB peak with the stock loader
58 / 61 GBsteady state after 4 chunks, no out-of-memory
~60 sper request for a 24-step chunk (~3 s on 2× H100)
~40 smodel load, 2,146 tensors, 0 missing

Graph-as-Policy × SO-101 on Jetson AGX Orin

Typed skill graph, all on one Orin

About

I got into robotics building an autonomous wheelchair on a Jetson Nano as an undergrad at Karunya. NVIDIA wrote about it in 2023, and I've been building on Jetson ever since.

These days most of my evenings go into robot learning. I bring robots up in Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab, collect demonstrations with leader arms and VR, train with parallel RL, fine-tune VLA and world-action models, and then try to get them running on edge hardware that wasn't designed for them. I publish the code and the numbers, including the runs that fail.

By day I work on autonomous tech at General Motors in Bangalore.
